# Sample questions to try

Simple:
- Show top 5 products by revenue last quarter
- How many orders were placed in 2025?
- What is the total revenue this year?

Aggregation / grouping:
- What is revenue by region?
- Which product category has the best profit margin?
- Show average order value by customer segment

Time-series (exercises the visualizer's line-chart path):
- What is the month-over-month revenue growth this year?
- Show daily order counts for the last 30 days

Window functions / ranking:
- Rank customers by total lifetime spend
- What is each product's revenue as a percentage of its category's total?

Multi-condition (exercises validator + schema retriever together):
- Which Enterprise customers in Europe placed more than 3 orders?
- Show refund rate by payment method

Deliberately ambiguous (tests the "default to revenue" prompt rule):
- Who are our top customers?
- What are the best-selling products?
